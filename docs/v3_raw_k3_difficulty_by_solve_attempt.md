## Metric Definitions

The structural metrics come from the V3 balanced level metadata generated by `sokoban_memory/boxoban.py`.

- `wall_density`: fraction of 10x10 board cells that are walls.
- `reachable_ratio`: `player_reachable_ratio`, the fraction of non-wall cells reachable from the initial player position while treating boxes as blocked.
- `initial_legal_pushes`: `initial_legal_push_count`, the number of legal box pushes available in the initial state.
- `difficulty_score`: weighted structural and solver-informed score:

```text
0.35 * (1 - player_reachable_ratio)
+ 0.25 * wall_density
+ 0.25 * (1 - min(initial_legal_push_count, 12) / 12)
+ 0.15 * solver_component
```

`solver_component` is `min(solver_min_pushes, 80) / 80` when the bounded solver found a solution; otherwise it defaults to `0.6`.

## Interpretation

The levels solved on the first attempt are structurally easier on average. They have the lowest average difficulty score, the highest number of initial legal pushes, and a higher reachable ratio than the retry-dependent or never-solved groups.

The levels solved on attempt 2 are more constrained: they have the highest average wall density, a lower reachable ratio, and only about five initial legal pushes on average. Raw same-level trajectory memory can rescue some of these levels, but it does not eliminate the local planning difficulty.

The never-solved group is not simply the highest-wall-density group. Its wall density is close to the attempt-3 group, but its reachable ratio is lower and its legal-push count remains low. Its average difficulty score is the highest of the four groups, suggesting that constrained maneuverability is more predictive than wall density alone.

Overall, under raw trajectory memory with `K = 3`, first-attempt success is associated with more open initial player mobility and more legal push options. Retry success appears mostly on levels with intermediate-to-high structural difficulty, while levels that remain unsolved combine low reachability with limited legal push choices.
